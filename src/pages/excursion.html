About Matobo Hills

Zimbabwe's Ancient Wonder

The Matobo Hills (also known as Matopos) is one of Zimbabwe's most extraordinary landscapes — a 3,100 km² expanse of granite kopjes, valleys, and caves that has been home to human settlement for over 13,000 years.

It is a UNESCO World Heritage Site, home to one of the world's highest concentrations of San Bushmen rock art, as well as sites of great historical significance like World's View and the Ntabazinduna hill. The park is also renowned for its white and black rhino populations — one of the best places in Africa to see rhino on foot.

What to Expect

  • Guided rhino tracking on foot in a safe conservation setting
  • Ancient San Bushmen rock art tours with expert guides
  • Stunning views over the granite kopje landscape
  • Visit to World's View & Ntabazinduna hill
  • Wildlife game drives: leopard, zebra, giraffe & more
  • Cultural exchange with local Ndebele guides — lunch served on site
