Things to Do

Explore the City of Kings

Bulawayo is one of Africa's most underrated destinations — rich with history, nature, and culture.

Matobo National Park

A UNESCO World Heritage Site featuring spectacular granite kopjes (balancing rocks), ancient San Bushmen rock art, and one of Africa's highest concentrations of white rhinos.

Natural History Museum

One of the largest natural history museums in Africa, home to an extraordinary collection of Zimbabwe's wildlife, geological specimens, and cultural artefacts including the famous Zimbabwe bird.

A unique collection of historic steam locomotives and railway history from the colonial era through Zimbabwe's independence — a fascinating piece of southern African industrial heritage.

Bulawayo Food & Culture Scene

The city is renowned for its warm hospitality and vibrant food culture. Explore local restaurants serving traditional Ndebele cuisine, street food markets, and the lively arts scene at the Zimbabwe Cultural Centre.

Travel

Getting to Bulawayo

Arrival and set-up day: Tuesday 29 June 2027. Pack a warm layer — July is cool in Bulawayo.

By Air

Joshua Mqabuko Nkomo International Airport (BUQ) serves Bulawayo with direct flights from Johannesburg (SA), Harare, and other regional hubs. Flight time from Johannesburg: ~1 hour 30 min.

By Road

Bulawayo is well connected by road. From Johannesburg, the drive via Beit Bridge is approximately 6–7 hours. From Harare, approximately 4–5 hours on the A5 highway.

By Rail

National Railways of Zimbabwe operates overnight train services connecting Bulawayo to Harare and Victoria Falls — a scenic and comfortable option for longer journeys.
