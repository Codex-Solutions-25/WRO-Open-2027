Africa AI Strategy Forum

A high-level breakfast dialogue shaping Africa's AI strategy, governance, digital economy & digital transformation agenda.

Friday 2 July 2027 08:00–11:30 · breakfast meeting ZITF Bulawayo 100 high-level delegates
Side Event 4

Shaping Africa's Digital Future

The Africa AI Strategy Forum brings together heads of government, ministers, technology executives, and policy-makers from across the continent for high-level dialogue on the most pressing questions in AI governance and digital transformation.

Discussions will focus on how African nations can harness artificial intelligence to accelerate economic growth, improve public services, and build resilient digital economies — ensuring Africa is not a passive consumer of AI but an active architect of it.

Key Topics

  • National AI governance frameworks for African contexts
  • Digital economy infrastructure and investment priorities
  • AI ethics, safety, and inclusive development
  • Cross-border data sharing and continental data markets
  • AI-driven transformation in education, health, and agriculture
