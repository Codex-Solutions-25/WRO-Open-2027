Africa's Robotics Marketplace

The Robotics Merchandise Exhibition creates a dedicated commercial space where leading robotics manufacturers, educational kit suppliers, and AI solution providers showcase their products directly to 200 African educators, school administrators, and distributors.

This is an unparalleled opportunity to place your products in front of the decision-makers who procure robotics and STEM resources for schools and institutions across the continent.

What's on Show

  • Educational robotics kits and platforms
  • AI hardware and embedded systems
  • STEM curriculum and coding resources
  • Competition-grade robotics components
  • African-made technology innovations
