About the Competition

Africa's First WRO
Special Invitational

The World Robot Olympiad is one of the world's premier robotics competitions — a network spanning 85+ countries and engaging 100,000+ students worldwide since 2014.

In 2027, Bulawayo, Zimbabwe becomes the proud host of Africa's first and largest WRO Special Invitational — an historic celebration of African talent in robotics, AI, and innovation for ages 8 to 22, implemented by Girls in STEM Trust Zimbabwe.
