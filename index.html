Competition Theme

Robotics Meets
Culture & Tech

In 2027, the Robotics Open Africa comes to Bulawayo, Zimbabwe. Teams will explore how robots can help shape, protect, and grow art and culture in today’s world.

As an open competition preparing for the international finals in South Africa, students will tackle advanced engineering and programming challenges.
