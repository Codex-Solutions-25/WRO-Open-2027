About the Competition

Africa's First WRO
Special Invitational

The World Robot Olympiad is a global robotics competition where young people design, build and programme robots to solve real-world challenges — a network spanning 85+ countries and engaging 100,000+ students worldwide since 2014.

In 2027, Bulawayo, Zimbabwe becomes the proud host of Africa's first and largest WRO Special Invitational — a dynamic showcase of youth-driven robotics and AI innovation, accelerating digital growth and unlocking young talent, implemented by Girls in STEM Trust Zimbabwe.
