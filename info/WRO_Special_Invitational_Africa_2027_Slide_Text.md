## Slide 1: World Robot Olympiad Special Invitational Africa 2027

**The World Robot Olympiad Competition**

This is one of the world's premier robotics competitions, featuring a network spanning over 85 countries and engaging more than 100,000 students worldwide since 2014. Its focus is on nurturing future-ready innovators, engineers, and entrepreneurs.

The competition has a proven history of promoting youth engagement in STEM, emerging technologies and fostering diversity.

**Africa's First**

This is the first WRO Special Invitational in Africa, to be hosted in Bulawayo, Zimbabwe, from the 1st to the 3rd of July 2027

**Africa's Largest**

This will be one of Africa's largest and innovative robotics competitions, promoting a celebration of talent from across the continent for ages 8 to 22

*Implementing Partner: Girls in STEM Trust Zimbabwe*

## Slide 2: World Robot Olympiad Special Invitational Africa Theme 2027

**The Future of Work - Dream it, Design it, Build it with Robots**

The theme focuses on equipping Africa's youth for a swiftly evolving world influenced by artificial intelligence, robotics, automation, and digital innovation.

- Dream It encourages young individuals to envision possibilities and tackle real-world challenges.
- Design It fosters creativity, critical thinking, and collaboration to transform ideas into actionable solutions.
- Build It with Robots empowers learners to utilize robotics, coding, and engineering skills to create, test, and enhance innovative technologies.

**What does the theme mean for our partners?**

This is a chance to invest in the future workforce of Africa, support innovation and entrepreneurship, promote inclusive STEM education, and engage with emerging talent. By backing the WRO Special Invitational Africa, partners play a vital role in cultivating the skills, ideas, and technologies that will propel Africa's digital economy forward.

## Slide 4: There is something for everyone ...

**A Multi-Stakeholder Innovation Experience**

Numerous events will be running parallel to the WRO Special Invitational competition and are open for partnership and sponsorship

**Side events**

- **Africa AI Strategy Forum** - a high level dialogue to shape Africa's AI strategy, governance, digital economy & digital transformation agenda. USD20,000 for event-naming rights and program co-creation package. Target attendance 100 high level delegates
- **AI & Robotics Educators Summit** - a platform to explore innovative approaches to integrating AI & robotics into Africa's education system. USD20,000 for event-naming rights and program development. Target attendance 200 African educators
- **Startup Ecosystem Support Network for WRO Meetup** - networking & collaboration platform connecting African startups, innovators, investors & industry leaders to showcase ideas, explore partnerships, & accelerate technology-driven solutions. The startups will be paired with competing students from their respective countries to provide post competition support for research and development as well as commercialization of innovations. Support required for 40 African Hub leads from 40 African countries to attend the event. USD20,000 for event-naming rights and program development. USD50 000 for travel and accomodation stipends for 40 African Hub leads. Target total attendees 150 startup leads, team coaches and potential partners
- **Robotics Merchandising Exhibition** - showcasing robotics products, educational kits, AI solutions, & related technologies, connecting exhibitors with buyers, educators, distributors & potential partners. USD20,000 event-naming rights and prime spot on program for direct audience address. Target attendance: 200 educators, school administrators and distributors.
