## 1. What is the WRO Special Invitational Africa?

The World Robot Olympiad is a global robotics competition where young people design, build and program robots to solve real-world challenges.

The WRO Association invites schools, learners, educators, robotics teams, tertiary institutions, exhibitors, sponsors and STEM organisations from across Africa to take part in the inaugural WRO Special Invitational Africa, hosted in Bulawayo, Zimbabwe by Girls in STEM Trust.

Teams explore how robotics, autonomous vehicles, IoT solutions, Artificial Intelligence and other emerging technologies can address real challenges facing African communities.

It is a platform to showcase creativity, build problem-solving skills, engage with emerging technology and connect with the wider STEM community.

### WRO stands for

1. Future-ready skills
2. STEM engagement
3. Emerging technologies
4. Diversity and inclusion

## 2. Competition Categories and Age Groups

### RoboStarter Innovators

*For those with little to no experience in robotics*

| Age group | Ages |
|---|---|
| Elementary | 8 to 12 |
| Junior | 13 to 15 |
| Senior | 16 to 18 |

The challenge guidelines will be shared with participants.

### Future Innovators

*Intermediate robotics skills*

| Age group | Ages |
|---|---|
| Elementary | 8 to 12 |
| Junior | 13 to 15 |
| Senior | 14 to 19 |

A project-based category. Teams study a real-world problem, come up with a robotics-based idea, and build a working prototype to present to judges.

### Future Engineers

*Advanced robotics experience*

| Age group | Ages |
|---|---|
| Junior | 14 to 18 |
| Senior | 19 to 22 |

A technology challenge where teams build and programme an autonomous robotic vehicle with free choice of hardware and programming language. The specific challenge will be shared with participants.

### Age group overview

| Category | Age group | Ages |
|---|---|---|
| RoboStarter Innovators | Elementary | 8–12 |
| RoboStarter Innovators | Junior | 13–15 |
| RoboStarter Innovators | Senior | 16–18 |
| Future Innovators | Elementary | 8–12 |
| Future Innovators | Junior | 13–15 |
| Future Innovators | Senior | 14–19 |
| Future Engineers | Junior | 14–18 |
| Future Engineers | Senior | 19–22 |

*Age groups follow the official WRO age bands. Eligibility is normally decided by year of birth, not age on the day. Organisers will confirm final age eligibility for the Special Invitational on registration.*

## 3. Competition Sub-themes

Teams choose a sub-theme and build a solution for it. Whatever your sector, your mission is on the competition floor.

1. **Agritech:** Food security and the future of farming
2. **Fintech:** Financial inclusion and digital finance
3. **HealthTech:** Better healthcare solutions for Africa
4. **Smart Cities & Communities:** Connected, sustainable places to live and work
5. **AI & Emerging Technologies:** Intelligent solutions for Africa's challenges
6. **Green Tech & Renewable Energy:** Climate action and clean energy

## 4. Application Process

Your journey to Bulawayo starts with a simple registration. We welcome schools, learners, educators and robotics teams from across Africa.

1. Complete the Expression of Interest & Early-Bird Registration Form.
2. Choose your category: RoboStarter Innovators, Future Innovators or Future Engineers.
3. Pay your registration fee before the deadline. Early-bird rates end 13 December 2026.
4. Receive official event updates and access information.
5. Choose any side events that interest you.
6. Travel to Bulawayo, compete, exhibit and celebrate.
